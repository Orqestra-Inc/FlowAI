You stay in control

Nothing moves money without your OK.

AI is fast, but it can be fooled like anyone else. So Flow works inside rules you set, and the important decisions always come back to a person.

  • Approvals on your phone. Orders and big payments wait for a tap from someone with authority.
  • Limits for each person. Your accountant can pay up to KSh 100,000; anything bigger comes to you.
  • Scam protection built in. "We've changed our bank details, pay now" messages get stopped, not paid.
  • A record of everything. See who asked for what, what Flow did, and who approved it.

FAQ

Good questions.

Will Flow replace my staff?

No. Flow takes the repetitive admin off your team's plate so they can spend time on customers, suppliers and growing the business. Most owners use it to grow without hiring for every new branch or product line.

Can Flow spend money without me knowing?

No. Orders and payments above each person's limit always wait for approval from someone with authority, and every action is recorded. You decide the limits.

Do I need to change my accounting software?

No. Flow works alongside what you already use. Today we set you up from your existing records (Excel, exports or even notebook photos). Direct connections to QuickBooks, Sage, Zoho and Odoo are rolling out with our first customers.

Does it work with M‑Pesa and eTIMS?

M‑Pesa matching and eTIMS-ready records are being built with our founding businesses now. Until then, Flow prepares payments for your team to make, and nothing is sent without you.

Is my business data safe?

Your data is only used to run your account. Each person only sees what their role allows, account numbers are masked, and we sign a data processing agreement with every customer in line with Kenya's Data Protection Act.

Does Flow understand Swahili?

Flow works in English today, and Swahili is on our list. You can mix in local names for products and suppliers and Flow will learn them.

What if I want to stop?

Cancel any time. Monthly plans stop at the end of the month, and we'll export your records for you.
