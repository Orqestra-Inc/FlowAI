FAQ

Questions owners ask us.

What does Orqestra actually do?

It does desk work the way a trained employee would: it reads your records and messages, follows your rules, does the work in your systems and asks a person when a decision is needed. Unlike a chatbot, it doesn't just talk; it gets things done.

Will it replace my staff?

No. It takes repetitive work off your team so they can spend time on customers, suppliers and growing the business. Most owners use it to grow without hiring for every new branch or product line.

Which features can I use today?

Stock & purchasing, Bills & payments and Reports & answers work now. Customer conversations, Sales follow-up and Team & admin are being built with our founding businesses, and they're included in your plan as they launch.

What counts as a task or a chat?

A back-office task is one job Orqestra does, like checking stock, drafting an order or preparing a payment. A customer chat is one customer’s conversation in a 24-hour window, however many messages it takes. Most small teams use a few hundred of each a month.

Can it spend money without me knowing?

No. Orders and payments above each person's limit wait for someone with authority, and every action is recorded. You set the limits.

Do I need to change my software?

No. Orqestra works alongside what you already use. We set you up from your existing records, and connect to your accounting software and M‑Pesa as those connections roll out.

Is my business data safe?

Your data is only used to run your account. Each person sees only what their role allows, account numbers are masked, and we sign a data processing agreement with every customer.
