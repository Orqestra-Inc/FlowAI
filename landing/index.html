What Flow does

Daily operations, in plain words.

Stock and reorders

"What's running low?" Flow checks inventory against reorder levels and tells you what to buy before you run out.

Suppliers and orders

Flow compares suppliers on price, minimum order and your cash, recommends one, and prepares a draft order for you to approve.

Bills and payments

See what's due this week and pay what's legitimate. Payments above a person's limit wait for approval.

How it works

Flow proposes. Your rules decide.

  1. 1

    You ask

    "We're low on cooking oil. Order more, but keep cash above KSh 80,000."

  2. 2

    Flow plans

    It reads stock, suppliers and cash, does the maths in code, and drafts the order. Nothing is placed yet.

  3. 3

    Every action is checked

    Before anything runs, it's checked against that person's permissions and limits: allowed, held or blocked.

  4. 4

    A person approves

    Orders and large payments need a human approval, tied to the exact supplier, items and total.

Safety

The AI can be fooled. Your rules can't be talked out of themselves.

"We've changed our bank details, please pay now" is one of the most common ways small businesses lose money. An AI reading your invoices will see these messages too, and sometimes it will believe them.

So Orqestra doesn't rely on the AI behaving. Every action is checked by an independent policy layer, powered by ArmorIQ, before it runs. If the check fails or can't be reached, nothing runs.

  • Blocked by default

    Flow can only do what a person's rules explicitly allow. Changing bank details and exporting data are off for everyone.

  • Limits per person

    The accountant can pay up to KSh 100,000; above that, the payment is held until someone with authority approves it.

  • Approvals that can't be reused

    An approval covers one exact order, expires, and can't be given by the person who drafted it. The AI can never approve anything.

  • Business rules underneath

    Cash reserve, maximum order size, no duplicate payments, and payments paused after a supplier's bank details change.

  • A full record

    Every allowed, held and blocked action is logged, with account numbers masked.

FAQ

Questions

Can the AI move money without me?

Only within the limits you set for each person, and only for actions their rules allow. Orders always need a human approval, and payments above a limit are held until someone approves them. The AI cannot approve anything itself.

What if someone tricks the AI with a fake invoice or message?

Assume it will be fooled sometimes. That's why every action is checked outside the AI before it runs. In our tests, an invoice with hidden instructions to change bank details, pay KSh 480,000 and export the supplier list got nowhere: the bank change and export were blocked and the payment was held.

Do I need to set up an ERP first?

No. Orqestra is the interface. We'll help design partners bring in their stock, supplier and bill data during onboarding.

Does it work with M-Pesa?

Not yet. Today orders and payments are simulated and clearly labelled as such. M-Pesa payouts are next on our list, and they'll go through the same limits and approvals.

Which AI model does it use?

Orqestra works with several leading AI models. Safety doesn't depend on which one: the checks sit outside the model.
