FAQ

Questions owners ask us.

Will Flow replace my staff?

No. Flow takes repetitive admin off your team so they can spend time on customers and suppliers. Most owners use it to grow without hiring for every new branch or product line.

Can Flow spend money without me knowing?

No. Orders and payments above each person's limit wait for someone with authority, and every action is recorded. You set the limits.

Do I need to change my accounting software?

No. We set you up from your existing records. Direct connections to QuickBooks, Sage, Zoho and Odoo are being built with our first customers.

Does it work with M‑Pesa and eTIMS?

Those connections are in progress with our founding businesses. Until then, Flow prepares payments for your team to make, and nothing is sent without you.

Is my business data safe?

Your data is only used to run your account. Each person sees only what their role allows, account numbers are masked, and we sign a data processing agreement with every customer.

What if I want to stop?

Cancel any time. Monthly plans end at the end of the month, and we export your records for you.
